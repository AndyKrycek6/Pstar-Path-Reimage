⌖ FIELD INSTRUCTION How to orientate yourself OPEN GUIDE +
ORIGINAL PILGRIM STAR PATH INSTRUCTION

Looking at the center, turn 177.02 degrees to your right.

The Galactic Centre is your reference north. Turn from that line toward the selected waypoint.
01

Enter your location. Use the four-block galactic address, then update the origin checkpoint.

02

Face the centre. Treat the Galactic Centre marker and the GC compass bearing as north.

03

Turn the displayed bearing. The route tells you how many degrees to turn and whether to turn left or right.

04

Check your vertical offset. Above or below shows the Y-axis difference; use the 3D map to inspect that height.

01

Set your origin

Enter a four-block hexadecimal address such as 0432:0076:0D66:0172, or use three decimal coordinates separated by commas.

02

Choose a destination

Use the destination selector or click a stored waypoint. The route telemetry and map markers update immediately.

03

Explore the projection

In 2D, drag to pan. In 3D, drag to tilt and rotate, or Shift-drag to pan. Scroll or use the map controls to zoom. For a slow automatic orbit, choose Free view, Player lock or Destination lock, then Start orbit. One revolution takes two minutes; dragging or leaving the map pauses it. Snap to User centres the map on your origin.

04

Keep the journey

Open Journeys to start a named flight record. Rename checkpoints or add notes below the origin status. Active journeys resume from their saved checkpoints, while completed journeys can be restored to replay their route in both map views.

BUILD NOTE

Local-first, expandable later.

Your saved settings and session state remain in this browser. Distances use the original Pilgrim Star Path scale of 400 LY per region. They are estimates between regions, not exact distances between stars. The black-hole ring is illustrative and does not predict an exit. Export backups in Config.

LOCAL DIRECTORY / NEW ENTRY

Add a destination

Give this destination a name, then enter a four-block signal-booster address or three whole-number coordinates (X/Z: 0–4095; Y: 0–255).

Example hex format: 0432:0076:0D66:0172
FLIGHT RECORD / NEW ARCHIVE

Start a new journey

Give this flight a name. Starting it closes the current journey at its last recorded stop. Every later origin update is saved as a checkpoint; choosing a target never records a visit.

Records stay local to this browser and can be replayed in the 3D map.
FLIGHT RECORD / CHECKPOINT EDIT

Rename checkpoint

Give this saved waypoint a name you will recognise when you return to this journey.

The new name is saved with this journey and appears on the route record.

Checkpoint note

Up to 240 characters. Clear the text and save to remove a note.

Restore this backup?

Export your current records first if you want to keep both sets. Restoring replaces saved data in this browser.
