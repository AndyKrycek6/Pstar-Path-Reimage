# Built-in capital destinations

Published references checked on 20 September 2026 for v0.40. All three destinations are in **Euclid**. These are published community coordinates, not an in-game verification.

| Community | Capital system | Signal-booster address | v0.40 change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Galactic Hub Project | HUB16-205 Bixiann (Uthmi Beta) | `041C:004F:0D89:0205` | Retained |
| Amino Hub | Amino Prime - AH | `064A:0082:01B9:0022` | Corrected system ID from `0051` to `0022` |
| Alliance of Galactic Travellers | AGT Embassy | `043D:0072:0D44:005F` | Replaced Apygen K50 / Rigusu with the capital |

## Sources and limits

- **Galactic Hub:** the community's [April 2026 event announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/NMSGalacticHub/comments/1sguapg/join_us_tomorrow_for_the_galactic_hubs_firstever/) identifies Bixiann / Uthmi Beta as its capital. A [July 2026 community report](https://www.reddit.com/r/NoMansSkyTheGame/comments/1uw0831/documented_griefer_infestation_griefed_the_portal/) supplies Uthmi Beta's portal address `2205D058AC1D`, which converts to the retained signal-booster address. The [community wiki](https://nmsgalactichub.miraheze.org/wiki/HUB16-205_Bixiann) could not be retrieved during this check; coordinate corroboration is therefore community-reported.
- **Amino Hub:** the community-catalogued [Amino Prime - AH system page](https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/Amino_Prime_-_AH) lists the capital and address `064A:0082:01B9:0022`. Its stated game-data update is Worlds Part I, 3 September 2024. This is the latest published system listing found; no newer in-game confirmation was available.
- **AGT:** the [official joining guide](https://www.nms-agt.com/team/joining-the-agt) gives AGT Embassy's address. The [civilization listing](https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_of_Galactic_Travellers) identifies it as the capital system, with AGT Nexus as the capital planet.

Only built-in destination references migrate. Custom waypoints and recorded journey checkpoints keep their original coordinates. The Pilgrim Star remains a separate historical reference.
